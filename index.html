How it works

Slide On. Snap In. Stand Out.

An interchangeable sleeve goes on and off your snapback strap in three simple steps.

01 / SLIDE ON

SLIDE ON

Slide the sleeve over the strap already on your snapback.

02 / SNAP IN

SNAP IN

Align the original pegs with the sleeve holes, then press the closure together until it snaps into place.

03 / STAND OUT

STAND OUT

Wear it, then switch sleeves whenever you want a different look.

Six sizes are numbered 0–5 by exposed pegs: size 0 means zero exposed pegs; size 5 means five exposed pegs.
Find your fit

Choose by exposed pegs.

Sizes 0–5 match the number of closure pegs visible when worn—not hat sizes. SnapInk Sleeves™ fit compatible standard plastic seven-peg snapbacks.
