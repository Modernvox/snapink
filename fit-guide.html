SnapInk™ Fit Guide

SnapInk™ sleeves are designed to fit hats with a standard plastic 7-peg snapback closure. Each sleeve slides over the existing female strap (the strap with adjustment holes), allowing the original snapback pegs to snap naturally into place. No modifications, replacement straps, or tools required.

SnapInk™

Not compatible with

  • Fitted hats
  • Buckle-style strapbacks
  • Velcro closures
  • Elastic closures
  • Nonstandard snapback closures

Choose your SnapInk size by exposed pegs

Sizes 0–5 are sleeve configurations, not hat sizes. Count the snapback pegs left exposed when you wear your hat and select the matching number.

Size 0 — 0 exposed pegs
Size 1 — 1 exposed peg
Size 2 — 2 exposed pegs
Size 3 — 3 exposed pegs
Size 4 — 4 exposed pegs
Size 5 — 5 exposed pegs

How to install a SnapInk sleeve

Step 1

SLIDE ON

Slide the sleeve over the female snapback strap, the strap with seven adjustment holes.

Step 2

SNAP IN

Align the original pegs with the female strap's adjustment holes, then press the closure together until the pegs snap into place.

Step 3

STAND OUT

Wear the finished sleeve on your favorite snapback and show off your design.

Want to try a design? Design your snapback strap sleeve in SnapInk Studio.

Still not sure?
