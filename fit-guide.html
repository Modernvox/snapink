SnapInk Fit Guide

Choose your SnapInk configuration by counting the pegs left exposed when you wear your snapback. The same system fits standard plastic seven-peg closures across hat brands.

Choose your fit by exposed pegs

Sizes 0–5 are sleeve configurations, not hat sizes. Select the number of pegs that remain visible when you wear your hat.

Universal fit for standard plastic 7-peg closures

SnapInk sleeves slide over the existing female strap, the side with seven adjustment holes. The original snapback closure stays in place.

Works with

Standard plastic seven-peg snapback closures, regardless of hat brand.

Not designed for

  • Fitted hats
  • Buckle-style strapbacks
  • Velcro or elastic closures
  • Nonstandard snapback closures

Slide on. Snap in. Ready to wear.

No modifications, replacement straps, or tools are required.

Step 1

Slide on

Slide the sleeve over the female snapback strap with seven adjustment holes.

Step 2

Snap in

Align the existing pegs with the holes and press the closure together.

Step 3

Wear and switch

Change sleeves whenever you want a different look on your favorite hat.
