Wholesale and custom branding

Custom snapback strap sleeves for your brand.

SnapInk sleeves add a customizable branding surface to the back of a snapback while helping protect its original plastic strap from everyday scratches, scuffs, and surface wear. The removable sleeve slides over the existing closure; it does not replace it.

Universal fit for standard plastic 7-peg snapback closures, regardless of hat brand. Select a sleeve configuration by the number of pegs exposed when the hat is worn.

6 complimentary blank sample sleeves

Approved pilot partners receive one blank sleeve in each size, 0–5, and choose either Standard or Billboard style for the full set. Samples support fit testing, material evaluation, and product demonstrations.

The wholesale minimum remains 50 sleeves, and mixed sizes are allowed within an order. Custom branding and production services are available separately.
